## Blocker: help and model gates can't pass in `--historical-product` mode

Two of the original gate tools check that the product was built at the current commit:

- `tools/check_help.py:32`: `product['source_commit']!=initial[0]`
- `tools/check_model_diagnostic.py:46`: the same check

So `secure_release_gate.py --historical-product --gate help|model` will always stop with "Integrated build differs". It fails safely, but those two gates can never pass. No other gate in `preference-gate-commands.json` compares the product commit to HEAD; the others only record it.

2. **Don't rerun help/model in historical mode.** Leave their results attributed to e005 and say so.

Do not make the checker report HEAD as the product commit. That would falsify where the product came from.
