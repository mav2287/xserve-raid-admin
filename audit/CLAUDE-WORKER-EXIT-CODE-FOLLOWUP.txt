**Refinements (not blockers)**
1. **Socket leak when the helper aborts after an abnormal cause.** If entry building, queue validation or a `ThreadDeath` aborts `exit` before the close step, `connection.close()` is skipped and `connected` stays true. `stopped` blocks any further use, but the socket only closes on GC. You could close in the outer catch when `cause!=null`.
2. **Any failure during entry preparation aborts the whole batch.** This includes an OOM while allocating a `BasicResponse` or exception: every `SyncSender` stays blocked and no async callback runs.
